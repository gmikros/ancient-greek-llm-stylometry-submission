METRICS EXPLAINED:

Silhouette Score:
- **Range**: -1 to +1
- **+1**: Perfect clustering (point far from other clusters, close to own)
- **0**: On decision boundary (ambiguous assignment)
- **-1**: Misclassified (closer to wrong cluster)
- **>0.5**: Strong, well-separated clusters
- **0.25-0.5**: Weak but acceptable structure
- **<0.25**: No substantial cluster structure

Calinski-Harabasz Index (CH):
- **Range**: 0 to ∞ (higher = better)
- **Formula**: Ratio of between-cluster to within-cluster variance
- **>100**: Well-separated clusters
- **>500**: Excellent separation
- **Use for**: Comparing different clustering schemes

Davies-Bouldin Index (DB):
- **Range**: 0 to ∞ (LOWER = better)
- **Formula**: Average similarity of each cluster to its most similar cluster
- **<1.0**: Good separation
- **<0.5**: Excellent separation
- **Use for**: Assessing cluster compactness vs separation

RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
1. Do Human and AI form well-defined, separated clusters?
2. Are system×prompt combinations cohesive (homogeneous within)?
3. Which grouping scheme best describes the data structure?
4. Are some conditions more "cloud-like" (cohesive) than others?

INTERPRETATION:

silhouette_scores.png:
- **Each bar** = Average silhouette for one condition
- **Colors**: Match system×prompt scheme (green, light/dark blue, light/dark red)
- **Gray dashed line**: Overall silhouette across all samples
- **Above overall line**: This condition is more cohesive than average
- **Below overall line**: This condition is more scattered

WHAT HIGH SILHOUETTE MEANS:
- Condition forms a tight, well-defined cluster
- Samples within condition are similar to each other
- Distinct from other conditions

WHAT LOW SILHOUETTE MEANS:
- Condition is scattered or overlaps with others
- High within-condition variability
- Ambiguous boundaries

cluster_separation_overview.png (3 panels):

Panel A - Calinski-Harabasz:
- Compare different clustering schemes
- Higher bar = better separation
- Use to identify: Which scheme has best-defined clusters?

Panel B - Davies-Bouldin:
- LOWER is better (opposite of CH)
- Shows cluster compactness
- Use to identify: Which scheme has tightest clusters?

Panel C - Silhouette:
- Combines separation and cohesion
- Most interpretable metric
- Use to identify: Overall best clustering scheme

EXPECTED PATTERNS:

Pattern 1: Human high silhouette, all AI conditions low
→ Human cluster is cohesive, AI is scattered
→ AI shows high variability, Human is consistent
→ **Interpretation**: "AI lacks consistent style signature"

Pattern 2: All conditions have high silhouette (>0.5)
→ Well-defined clusters for each condition
→ System×prompt combinations are distinct
→ **Interpretation**: "Each configuration produces recognizable style"

Pattern 3: Claude-Restricted highest, GPT-Free lowest
→ Claude-Restricted most cohesive (consistent outputs)
→ GPT-Free most scattered (variable outputs)
→ **Interpretation**: "Restricted prompts increase consistency"

Pattern 4: Binary (Human vs AI) has higher scores than detailed schemes
→ Main division is Human vs AI
→ Within-AI subdivisions less meaningful
→ **Interpretation**: "System and prompt effects smaller than Human-AI gap"

PRACTICAL IMPLICATIONS:

High Human silhouette, low AI silhouette:
→ Human authors have consistent styles
→ AI outputs are heterogeneous
→ Need to improve AI consistency

High Claude-Restricted silhouette:
→ This configuration produces consistent outputs
→ Reliable, predictable behavior
→ Good for production use

Low variability across conditions:
→ All AI outputs similar
→ System/prompt choice doesn't matter much
→ Focus on other factors
