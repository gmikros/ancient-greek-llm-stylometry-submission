ANALYSIS 20: CLAUSE COMPLEXITY & SYNTACTIC PACKAGING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT THIS SHOWS:
- Clause-level syntactic complexity beyond mere sentence length
- Whether AI differences reflect "syntactic packaging" vs verbosity
- Subordination vs coordination patterns
- Clause density (clauses per sentence)

VARIABLES INVOLVED:
- avg_clause_length: Average tokens per clause
- subordination_ratio: Proportion of subordinate clauses
- pos_subordinating_conjunction: Frequency of subordinating conjunctions
- pos_coordinating_conjunction: Frequency of coordinating conjunctions
- Category1 (System), Category3 (Prompt), Category4 (Label)

FILES GENERATED:
- clause_features_global.png - 2×2 panel: distributions (Human vs AI)
- clause_stats_global.csv - Mean, std, median by Human vs AI
- clause_effect_sizes.csv - Cohen's d for each clause feature
- clause_all_features_by_system_prompt.png - **COMBINED 2×2 panel** (all features)
- clause_[feature]_by_system_prompt.png - Individual bar plots per feature
- clause_by_system_prompt.csv - Full system×prompt breakdown
- clause_vs_sentence_length.png - Clause vs sentence comparison + density

RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
1. Do AI and Human differ in syntactic packaging (not just verbosity)?
2. Does AI use more/less subordination than coordination?
3. Do Restricted prompts compress clause structure toward Human?
4. Does Claude vs GPT differ in clause-level syntax?
5. Is sentence length increase due to MORE clauses or LONGER clauses?

INTERPRETATION:

Avg Clause Length:
- Human > AI: AI uses shorter clauses (simpler internal structure)
- AI > Human: AI inflates clause-internal complexity

Subordination Ratio:
- Human > AI: Human uses more embedding (complex syntax)
- AI > Human: AI over-subordinates (excessive embedding)
- Similar values: Syntactic packaging is preserved

Clause Density (derived: sentence_len / clause_len):
- AI > Human: More clauses per sentence (packaging difference)
- Human > AI: More clauses in human text
- Similar: Sentence length reflects clause length, not clause count

CROSS-LEVEL SYNTHESIS:

Scenario 1: AI longer sentences + similar clause length + higher clause density
→ AI adds MORE clauses (true packaging difference)

Scenario 2: AI longer sentences + longer clauses + similar clause density
→ AI inflates WITHIN clauses (verbosity, not structural)

Scenario 3: AI longer sentences + longer clauses + lower clause density
→ AI uses FEWER but much LONGER clauses (simplified syntax)

PRACTICAL IMPLICATIONS:
- Addresses "syntactic packaging vs word-form complexity" narrative
- Shows if Free/Restricted prompts affect structural complexity
- Reveals whether sentence length differences are superficial or deep
- Guides prompt engineering: target clause-level vs word-level features

ROBUSTNESS CHECKS:

Together, they separate:

⚠️ INTERPRETATION CAVEAT:
Clause segmentation in Ancient Greek is non-trivial:
- Participial phrases may/may not count as clauses
- Accusative + infinitive constructions are ambiguous
- Expect some noise in clause boundaries

However, systematic differences between Human and AI are still meaningful
even if absolute clause counts have measurement error.
