ANALYSIS 16: AUTHOR PROXIMITY BY SYSTEM × PROMPT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT THIS SHOWS:
- STRATIFIED proximity: which system×prompt combinations preserve 
  style most faithfully
- Interaction between system and prompt in style preservation
- Condition-specific fidelity to originals

VARIABLES INVOLVED:
- Category1 (System): Claude, GPT
- Category2 (Author): 10 authors
- Category3 (Prompt): Free, Restricted
- All numeric features

CALCULATION:
For each system × prompt:
1. For each author, get human centroid (same as #15)
2. For AI texts with this system+prompt, compute distances
3. Average across all authors

FILES GENERATED:
- author_centroid_distance_long.csv - Distance per text
- author_centroid_distance_mean_by_sys_prompt.csv - Mean by condition
- author_centroid_distance_heatmap.png - Heatmap (systems × prompts)
