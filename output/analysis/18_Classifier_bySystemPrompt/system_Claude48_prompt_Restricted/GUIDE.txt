ANALYSIS 17: CLASSIFIER (GLOBAL)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT THIS SHOWS:
- Overall detectability of AI-generated texts
- How well machine learning can distinguish Human from AI
- Classifier calibration (are probabilities trustworthy?)

VARIABLES INVOLVED:
- Category4 (Label): Human (0) vs AI (1)
- All ~107 numeric features
- Optional: Category2 (Author) for grouped cross-validation

CALCULATION:
1. Cross-validation:
   - If author available: GroupKFold (no author leakage)
   - Otherwise: StratifiedKFold (5-fold)
2. Per fold:
   - Fit StandardScaler on training data only
   - Fit LogisticRegression on scaled training data
   - Predict probabilities on scaled test data
3. Evaluate: ROC curve, AUROC, calibration, Brier score

FILES GENERATED:
- roc_curve.png - ROC curve with AUROC (includes classifier & CV method)
- calibration_curve.png - Calibration plot (includes classifier & CV method)
- roc_calibration_combined.png - **COMBINED** (ROC + Calibration side-by-side)
- prediction_distribution.png - **NEW** (histogram of predictions by true label)
- cv_probs.csv - Predicted probabilities per text
- metrics.json - AUROC, Brier, CV method, classifier name, sample size

RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
1. Can we reliably distinguish AI from Human based on style?
2. Is there a clear stylistic boundary?
3. Are predicted probabilities trustworthy?

INTERPRETATION:
AUROC (Area Under ROC Curve):
- 1.0: Perfect classification (never in practice)
- 0.9-1.0: Excellent (strong AI signature)
- 0.8-0.9: Good (detectable differences)
- 0.7-0.8: Fair (subtle differences)
- 0.5: Random guessing (no signal)

Calibration Curve:
- On diagonal: Well-calibrated (predicted = actual)
- Above diagonal: Under-confident
- Below diagonal: Over-confident

Brier Score:
- 0: Perfect probabilistic predictions
- < 0.1: Excellent calibration
- 0.25: Random guessing

PRACTICAL IMPLICATIONS:
- High AUROC: AI detectable → authenticity verification possible
- Low AUROC: AI mimics well → impressive generation quality
- Poor calibration: Don't trust probabilities literally (but ranking OK)

⚠️ LIMITATION:
Aggregates all AI (both systems, prompts, authors). Doesn't tell you:
- If Claude is more detectable than GPT
- If Free prompts are more detectable than Restricted

INTERPRETATION:

Prediction Distribution:
- **Good separation**: Green clustered left (0-0.3), Red clustered right (0.7-1.0)
  → Classifier confidently distinguishes groups
  → Few ambiguous cases
  
- **Moderate overlap**: Some green near 0.5, some red near 0.5
  → Classifier uncertain for some texts
  → Harder classification task
  
- **Poor separation**: Distributions heavily overlap
  → Classifier struggles
  → Low AUROC expected
