ANALYSIS 21: LEXICAL RICHNESS (GLOBAL)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT THIS SHOWS:
- Lexical diversity and vocabulary richness
- Global comparison: Human vs AI
- Optional: System comparison (Claude vs GPT)
- Whether AI maintains lexical sophistication

VARIABLES INVOLVED:
- TTR (Type-Token Ratio): Unique words / total words
- MATTR (Moving-Average TTR): Rolling window TTR (more robust)
- Hapax legomena: Words appearing only once (lexical creativity)
- n_unique_words: Total vocabulary size
- Category1 (System): Claude vs GPT (if analyzed)

RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
1. Does AI maintain lexical diversity comparable to humans?
2. Is AI more repetitive (lower TTR) or more varied (higher TTR)?
3. Does AI generate hapax legomena (lexical creativity)?
4. Do Claude and GPT differ in lexical richness?

INTERPRETATION:

TTR (Type-Token Ratio):
- Range: 0 to 1 (higher = more diverse vocabulary)
- Human > AI: Humans use richer vocabulary
- AI > Human: AI over-diversifies (unusual)
- Similar: Vocabulary diversity preserved
- Typical values: 0.4-0.7 for literary texts

MATTR (Moving-Average TTR):
- More robust than TTR (controls for text length)
- Same interpretation as TTR
- Preferred metric for comparing texts of different lengths

Hapax Legomena (words appearing once):
- High hapax rate: Lexical creativity, rich vocabulary
- Low hapax rate: Repetitive, limited vocabulary
- Human > AI: Humans more creative/varied
- AI > Human: AI less coherent (possible over-generation)

n_unique_words:
- Absolute vocabulary size
- Confounded with text length
- Use with caution or normalize by text length

CROSS-REFERENCE WITH ANALYSIS #03:
From sentence/word length analysis:
- If word length similar BUT lexical richness differs →
  AI uses same-length words but more repetitively
- If both differ → comprehensive lexical difference

EXPECTED PATTERNS:

Scenario 1: Human TTR > AI TTR
→ AI is more repetitive, reuses vocabulary
→ Lacks lexical creativity
→ **Interpretation**: "AI maintains grammatical structure but shows lexical poverty"

Scenario 2: Human TTR < AI TTR
→ AI over-diversifies vocabulary
→ Possible lack of coherence
→ **Interpretation**: "AI generates varied but incoherent vocabulary"

Scenario 3: TTR similar, hapax different
→ Overall diversity similar but creativity differs
→ Focus on hapax for lexical innovation measure

BY SYSTEM COMPARISON:

If Claude TTR > GPT TTR:
→ Claude uses richer vocabulary
→ May be more creative but less focused

If GPT TTR > Claude TTR:
→ GPT more diverse
→ Check if coherent or scattered

PRACTICAL IMPLICATIONS:
- Low AI TTR → Prompts should encourage lexical variation
- High AI hapax → May indicate hallucination/errors (check manually)
- System differences → Choose based on desired lexical profile

ROBUSTNESS CONSIDERATIONS:
- TTR decreases with text length (longer texts → lower TTR)
- MATTR controls for this (preferred)
- Hapax rate should be normalized by text length
- Compare within similar-length texts when possible
