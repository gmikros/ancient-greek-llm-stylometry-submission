ANALYSIS 19: COMPREHENSIVE JSD VISUALIZATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT THIS SHOWS:
- Cross-family comparison of JSD (mood vs case vs particle)
- Author ranking by overall stylistic preservation
- Correlation patterns across linguistic dimensions
- Multi-dimensional author profiles

VARIABLES INVOLVED:
- Category2 (Author): 10 Ancient Greek authors
- Category4 (Label): Human vs AI
- Feature families: mood, case, particle

FILES GENERATED:
- js_by_author_all_families.csv - JSD for all families per author
- js_author_ranking.png - Authors ranked by average JSD (horizontal bar)
- js_by_family_per_author.png - Grouped bars (3 families per author)
- js_correlation_across_families.png - Correlation heatmap
- js_correlation_matrix.csv - Correlation values
- js_mood_vs_case_scatter.png - 2D scatter with author labels

RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
1. Which authors are hardest/easiest to mimic overall?
2. Are mood, case, and particle equally difficult to preserve?
3. If an author is difficult in one dimension, are they difficult in all?
4. Do different feature families show independent patterns?

INTERPRETATION:

Author Ranking Plot (js_author_ranking.png):
- Lower values = better AI preservation of style
- Higher values = AI struggles with this author
- Use to identify outlier authors (very easy/hard to mimic)

Feature Family Comparison (js_by_family_per_author.png):
- 3 bars per author (orange=mood, blue=case, purple=particle)
- If bars are similar height → consistent difficulty across families
- If one bar much taller → specific feature family is problematic
- Pattern across authors reveals systematic weaknesses

Correlation Matrix (js_correlation_across_families.png):
- High correlation (>0.7): Families behave similarly
  → Authors difficult in mood are also difficult in case
  → Suggests general "complexity" factor
- Low correlation (<0.3): Families are independent
  → Mood difficulty ≠ case difficulty
  → Suggests feature-specific preservation

Mood vs Case Scatter (js_mood_vs_case_scatter.png):
- Points above diagonal: Case harder than Mood for this author
- Points below diagonal: Mood harder than Case
- Points near diagonal: Equally difficult
- Clusters reveal author groups with similar patterns

PRACTICAL IMPLICATIONS:

High Correlation Scenario:
→ Authors have general "imitability" factor
→ Focus on overall style preservation strategies
→ Difficult authors need comprehensive prompt engineering

Low Correlation Scenario:
→ Feature families are independently preserved
→ Can target specific families (e.g., improve mood without affecting case)
→ Flexible prompt engineering possible
