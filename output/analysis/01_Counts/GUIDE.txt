WHAT THIS SHOWS:
- Sample sizes for each condition
- Balance of your experimental design
- Which author-system-prompt combinations exist in your data

- Category1 (System): Claude, GPT, Human
- Category2 (Author): Aeschines, Andocides, Antiphon, Demosthenes, 
                      Dinarchus, Hyperides, Isaeus, Isocrates, Lycurgus, Lysias

FILES GENERATED:

- cat1_counts.csv/png - Distribution by System
- cat2_counts.csv/png - Distribution by Author

- *_crosstab.csv/png - Two-way combinations

RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
1. Is my dataset balanced? Do I have similar numbers across conditions?
2. Which author × system × prompt combinations are present?
3. Does my data structure match my intended experimental design?

INTERPRETATION TIPS:
- Look for empty cells in crosstabs (missing combinations)
- Check for severe imbalances that might affect statistical power
- Verify all 10 authors have both originals and AI rewrites
- Minimum 20-30 samples per author recommended

NEXT STEPS:
- If imbalanced: Consider stratified sampling or weighted analyses
- If missing combinations: Adjust research questions accordingly
- Proceed to Analysis #02 for descriptive statistics
