ANALYSIS 03: SENTENCE & WORD LENGTH DISTRIBUTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT THIS SHOWS:
- Probability density of sentence lengths and word lengths
- Whether AI rewrites preserve length structure at both levels

VARIABLES INVOLVED:
- Sentence length feature: avg_sentence, mean_sentence, sent_len, or tokens_per_sentence
- Word length feature: avg_word, mean_word, word_len, avg_word_length, or mean_word_length
- Category4 (Label): Human vs AI grouping

FILES GENERATED:
- distribution_[sentence_column].png - Sentence length histograms
- distribution_[word_column].png - Word length histograms (if available)

RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
1. Do AI systems preserve sentence length structure?
2. Do AI systems preserve word length patterns?
3. Are there systematic differences in length distributions?
4. Do the distributions overlap or diverge?

INTERPRETATION TIPS:

Sentence Length:
- Overlapping distributions: AI preserves sentence structure well
- AI shifted right: AI uses longer sentences
- AI shifted left: AI uses shorter sentences
- Different variance: AI is more/less consistent

Word Length:
- Overlapping distributions: AI preserves lexical complexity well
- AI shifted right: AI uses longer/more complex words
- AI shifted left: AI uses shorter/simpler words
- Different variance: AI has more/less lexical variety

PRACTICAL IMPLICATIONS:
- Reveals if AI maintains syntactic complexity (sentence level)
- Shows if AI preserves lexical sophistication (word level)
- Suggests if prompts need length constraints
- Identifies basic stylistic differences at multiple levels

CROSS-LEVEL ANALYSIS:
- If sentence length differs but word length matches → AI changes sentence structure but not vocabulary
- If word length differs but sentence length matches → AI changes vocabulary but not syntax
- If both differ → comprehensive stylistic shift
- If neither differs → strong preservation of surface structure
