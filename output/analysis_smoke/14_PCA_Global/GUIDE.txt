ANALYSIS 14: PCA SCATTER (GLOBAL)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT THIS SHOWS:
- Overall stylometric "space" of your texts
- Separation between Human and AI texts
- Clustering patterns and outliers

VARIABLES INVOLVED:
- All ~107 numeric features
- Category4 (Label): Human vs AI (color coding)
- PC1 and PC2: First two principal components

CALCULATION:
1. Standardize all features
2. Perform PCA (Principal Component Analysis)
3. Project each text onto PC1 and PC2
4. Plot with colors for Human vs AI

FILES GENERATED:
- pca_scatter_global.png - Human vs AI (basic view)
- pca_scatter_by_system.png - Colored by System (Human, Claude, GPT)
- pca_scatter_by_prompt.png - Colored by Prompt (Human, Free, Restricted)
- pca_composite_three_views.png - **COMPOSITE** (all three in one figure)

RESEARCH QUESTIONS:
1. Do Human and AI texts form distinct clusters?
2. How much do distributions overlap?
3. Are there unusual texts (outliers)?
4. What % of variance is explained by PC1-PC2?

INTERPRETATION:
Separation:
- Distinct clusters: Strong AI signature, easily distinguishable
- Overlapping clouds: Some AI texts are very human-like
- PC1 explained variance > 30%: Single dominant dimension

Outliers:
- Far from Human cluster: Atypical authors or errors
- Far from AI cluster: Failed rewrites or unusual prompts

LIMITATIONS:
- Only shows 2 dimensions (typically 20-40% of variance)
- Color-coded by overall Human/AI, not system or prompt
- Linear method (may miss nonlinear patterns)

ADDITIONAL VIEWS (STRATIFIED):

- Claude and GPT separate → systems have distinct "styles"

PCA by Prompt (pca_scatter_by_prompt.png):
- **Colors**: Green (Human), Red (Free), Blue (Restricted)
- **Markers**: Circle (Human), Triangle (Free), Square (Restricted)
- **Shows**: Whether Free and Restricted prompts cluster differently
- **Use to identify**: Prompt strategy effect on overall style

Interpretation:
- Free and Restricted overlap → prompt doesn't affect overall style
- Restricted closer to Human → prompt compresses toward human style
- Free scattered → prompt increases variability
